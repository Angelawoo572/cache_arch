## Sending the previous LBM rule to Google

This separate diagnostic keeps LBM R1's published counts, action menu and one-in-eight grants. It evaluates the first subsequent use of a proposed line, within 64 ingresses and 4,096 cycles, at the donor's 561-cycle service reference. In A, the current-PC/offset rule finds 51 distinct qualified original misses also missed by all three native controls across seven Google cases, exactly the same targets as the donor's unconditional action prior. In C it finds 128 versus 126 (five gained, three lost). Prior-page context finds 51 in A and 125 in C. These are marginal targets on a frozen execution. [Score definitions](frozen_protocol.md) · [Every recording and control](frozen_lbm.csv)

An apparent B-window success warrants scrutiny. On Google Tahoe the current-PC/offset table reaches 217 such targets; 74 others are covered exclusively by the unconditional prior. Of 767 common slots, 742 match trained mixed PC/offset buckets, but zero have the same full PC/offset pair as the LBM donor. As an exploratory sensitivity, relabel every PC by a fixed XOR while preserving equality, dynamic order, addresses and times. All 15 alternative labelings reach 74 targets. A same-page fixed +16 reaches 358, and +32 reaches 253 on the original slots. These are post-hoc matched controls; they establish that numeric PC labels and simple spatial structure suffice to change the result. No evidence here establishes semantic transfer between the two programs or higher IPC. The donor's previously measured performance on LBM remains a separate result.
