# What the four native prefetchers do on the same discovery windows

Across the seven Google discovery prefixes, NoPF demand LLC MPKI ranges from **3.472 to 21.732**. A real difference between conventional successes emerges in Google Delta. The same physical line can be forecast from several PCs using different displacements. Which producer acts, and how its request interacts with other loads, matters even where both predictors know the right address. [Per-policy metrics](performance.csv) · [Paired outcome changes](paired_outcomes.csv)

## A real example from interleaved PCs

In Delta, associate physical line 58,158 with the same demand, instruction 100,121, operand 0 at PC 60,342,688. Stride's previous observation of that PC was instruction 100,036 at line 58,156. It proposed +2 lines, filled at cycle 55,876 and beat demand ingress at 55,938. SPP used instruction 100,113 from PC 60,342,644 at line 58,157 to propose +1. Its proposal at 55,923 preceded *its own* demand ingress by two cycles. It filled at 56,133, after demand had merged, and this load accumulated 187 measured ROB-head wait cycles. The example was selected after seeing the aggregate, as the earliest operand in the largest repeated producer/consumer and displacement combination. Each clock belongs to its own execution. The producer instruction order comes from the underlying trace. Source-level function and stream semantics are unknown. [Exact proposal, dispatch, fill and demand identities](producer_examples.csv)

Of 1,786 original misses that Stride turned into a timely hit in this window, SPP retained 914 and turned 872 into late merges. For 866 of those 872, a same-consumer-PC +2 prediction under Stride competed with a different-PC +1 prediction under SPP. All 872 of Stride's producers occurred earlier in dynamic instruction order. Median time from proposal to demand ingress fell from 122 cycles under Stride to two under SPP, while median proposal-to-fill changed from 98 to 90. Median per-operand ROB-head waits were zero and 67 cycles. These load-level medians summarize the selected cohort; the whole-run cycle change includes all demands. [Grouped producer counts](producer_pairs.csv) · [Pairwise medians](paired_outcomes.csv)

The exchange also goes in the other direction. SPP converted 713 Stride-late loads and 194 previously persistent loads into direct timely hits. The same +2 versus +1 producer relation occurred on all 713 converse conversions. Overall SPP obtained **35 more** direct timely hits than Stride but used **72,535 more** cycles (326,287 vs 253,752) with 192 more total DRAM bytes. Multi-delta finished in 247,619 cycles. This workload supports preserving PC-local successes and evaluating each predictor on a complete execution. It supplies no hardware-cost advantage for a learned model. The second interval and deeper Google measurement test how often the result survives a changed window. [Native totals and timing](performance.csv)

## Could a frozen conditional forecast have acted before the native predictor?

We matched all eight two-future examples exported by the A→B transfer test to their cue and target operand IDs in each of the three live policy streams. All 24 cue/target joins were present. The audit regenerates the prefix key and addresses the frozen A table at each arm's actual cue, matches proposals since the previous same-line demand ingress, and records the accepted first-use transaction through dispatch, fill and demand. A physical target is representable at a cue when the registered vocabulary and known-page rule allow it. Cache residency and the issue queue can still block admission. [Native cue and target ledger](transfer_witnesses.csv)

- **MCF B, offset 19 at PC 4,213,196 with consecutive same-PC +1 deltas.** For the +2 branch the target at instruction 761,956 was already timely under Stride, multi-delta and SPP. For the +6 branch, instruction 791,853 on line 190,931 identified line 190,937, consumed by instruction 792,003. Each live stream retained the same frozen ordered-PC key and allowed the same-page target **1,354 cycles before demand ingress**. None of the three produced an observed matching proposal in the interval through demand lookup. All three missed, with 238 cycles of measured operand-head wait each. The +6 choice had one positive example among five occurrences of its training key. These observations establish an opportunity for a replay. Its marginal cycle savings, and the utility of other offers made by this rule, remain open. [Training support](../transfer/paired_futures.csv)
- **MCF B, offset 34.** For instruction 783,334 every conventional policy did eventually name the correct line. All three issued two cycles before demand ingress and filled 208 cycles after it. The frozen +2 cue had 1,106 cycles of lead on Stride and 1,181 on the other arms. Its target recorded zero operand-head wait in all three. At instruction 757,777, the +5 opportunity seen on the Stride reference trajectory lost its frozen prediction in the live multi-delta and SPP histories. Same input operands do not guarantee identical prediction contexts. [Per-policy timing](transfer_witnesses.csv)
- **Google Charlie, separate within-deep-window probe.** Instruction 4,658,501, PC 887,371,824, on line 370,560 occurred five demand ingresses and 104 cycles before the load of 370,565 in all three policy runs. The 99-cycle screening threshold used a training-period dispatch-to-fill reference. Native multi-delta proposed the same line 26 cycles after the cue and filled 132 cycles after target ingress. SPP proposed 91 cycles after the cue and filled 236 cycles after ingress. Their actual dispatch-to-fill intervals were 190 and 229 cycles, plus 20 cycles from proposal to dispatch. Stride never proposed the target and its demand took 203 cycles from dispatch to fill. The target's head waits on Stride/multi-delta/SPP were 20/0/17 cycles. No earlier data-demand ingress or prefetch proposal on that line appears in the ROI by the cue; the logs lack a full pre-cue residency probe. The five-cycle margin above the screening threshold therefore supplies no evidence that a real request would have arrived in time. The forecast was tested within a discovery trace, separately from the A→B experiment. ## Compatibility and interpretation

The DPC4 distributor describes the Google inputs as ChampSim conversions of the public V2 recordings. Google's upstream V2 synthetic ISA explicitly provides register identifiers, dependency information and instruction categories. The V1 discussion about missing dependencies applied to a previous release. The DPC4 trace input includes two destination and four source registers, two destination and four source memory operands, branch flags, and the instruction PC. Our C++ probe includes the actual build's input type and checks its size, seven offsets and host endianness. The checks establish how these on-disk fields reach Pythia. They cannot recover original opcodes or audit dependencies omitted, truncated, folded or relabeled in an upstream conversion. Quantitative retirement waits and IPC are predictions of this model. [DPC4 trace provenance](https://github.com/CMU-SAFARI/DPC4#workload-traces) · [Google V2 format](https://dynamorio.org/google_workload_traces.html) · [DPC4 ChampSim input struct](https://github.com/CMU-SAFARI/ChampSim/blob/master/inc/trace_instruction.h) · [Earlier converter discussion](https://github.com/ChampSim/ChampSim/pull/379)

We ran the native controls on Arizona first and checked every original ROI load against all four simulator CORE logs before expanding the screen. The full validation reconstructed 1,044,025 original load operands once across the 16 source windows, checked all four copies including operand slots and virtual lines, and verified each of the **253,752** LLC ingresses across the 64 arms against the input PC, original operand and policy's physical page map. All complete per-case maps were identical across policies. Input addresses are treated as virtual by the simulator and remapped into its physical 4 KiB frames. The machine has a 32 KiB L1D, 256 KiB L2, and 4 MiB LLC from the two-core geometry with one active core. This is the project's machine for comparability with SPEC/GAP; official DPC4 defaults use their own active prefetchers. [Checks by case and arm](identity_audit.csv) · [Input field counts](../intake/format_audit.csv) · [Native build](../../sim/README.md) · [DPC4 simulation configurations](https://sites.google.com/view/dpc4-2026/submit-work/infrastructure)

The only online trigger is a data-load ingress at the physical LLC interface. Retired instruction identifiers, paired NoPF tag outcomes, future demand line, first-use attribution and ultimate head wait are retrospective. The issuer performs the common same-page, residency, MSHR and queue checks. Useful accuracy reports first-use timely hits and late merges divided by accepted requests; timely accuracy uses timely first uses alone. Timely coverage divides paired attributed timely uses by original NoPF demand tag misses. LLC and L2 tag rates use demand tag lookups at that level. Average queue and MSHR occupancies are cycle-weighted. Unresolved requests stay in the denominator, especially relevant in these short windows. A request ultimately evicted without a tracked use is visible; a victim's lost residency, and the counterfactual performance cost of its displacement, are not attributed in this screening. Completion cycles and IPC use the same retired work. Unique ROB-head load wait counts are diagnostics; per-load waits can overlap. ## Reproduction

From `llc_hybrid_prefetch`, prepare the registered discovery prefixes from [intake](../intake/) and build the [archived conventional simulator](../../opportunity_study/conventional/conventional_baselines.md). Use a Python interpreter with NumPy and pandas.

```sh
mkdir -p workload_expansion/raw/screen
c++ -std=c++14 -I opportunity_study/raw/conventional/build/pythia/inc workload_expansion/screen/record_abi.cc -o workload_expansion/raw/screen/check_record
workload_expansion/raw/screen/check_record
python3 -B workload_expansion/screen/run.py
python3 -B workload_expansion/screen/history.py
python3 -B workload_expansion/screen/measure.py
python3 -B workload_expansion/screen/pairwise.py
python3 -B workload_expansion/screen/producers.py
```

Alternate windows pass `--skip`, `--warmup`, `--roi`, `--prefix-dir` and a distinct `--output` to the runner. The analyzers use the measured window and trace path in each native run and support `--case` filters. Place alternate compact outputs under a distinct `--summaries` directory. For a clean rerun the original input data, native compiler and checked-out simulator remain prerequisites. No held-out trace is accepted by these entry points.
