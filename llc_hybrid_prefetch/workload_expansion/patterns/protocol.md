# Whole-stream pattern discovery protocol

Register these tests before reading any screening outcomes. Read only the positive discovery rows in the intake registry. Held-out workload families have no role in this analysis. The principal clock and physical history for address prediction are the one-core Stride execution. The coverage labels of NoPF, Stride, multi-delta and SPP come from matching dynamic inputs in each native execution.

1. Count every LLC ingress, including upper-path and unpaired outcomes when a policy's visible stream changes. Describe continuous at-least-two timely first-use hits, their continuations and first failures in whole-core, PC-local and physical-page order. Separately count PCs with no timely first uses and their traffic, native misses and head-exposed misses. For an interleaving marker, count consecutive global PC switches and returns to the same PC following other PCs. Report when the same PC's physical delta repeats on return.
2. At every Stride ingress attempt to name the 1st, 4th or 16th subsequent physical ingress line, or the next same-PC line within 64 intervening positions. Score each task separately. For prefetch credit the chosen demand must be the first subsequent access to that line since the cue, distinct from the cue's physical line. No retrospective miss filtering at the decision point. Report all targets, targets whose NoPF lookup missed, targets reached by each native policy and remaining targets on the same IDs.
3. Compare information already visible at the cue. The local key is current full PC, exact page offset and clipped previous same-PC physical delta. Compare local alone, local plus current global delta, local plus one immediately preceding PC, and local plus an ordered three-PC prefix. A permutation-invariant three-PC multiset controls for order. Address-only and PC-identity-free relative keys provide controls for dependence on PC identity. A conventional next-line, PC-local repeated stride and a physical-address successor table give independent reference points. Each fit is a counted top-one choice for at most 256 contexts, minimum three training occurrences, frozen for evaluation, with abstention on unknown keys. Each key predicts a relative action; exact temporal maps seen physical lines to physical successors.
4. Legal output menu in priority order: a signed displacement up to 63 lines on the current physical page, any line on the most recent or second-most-recent distinct previously observed physical pages, then a signed physical displacement of ±64, ±128, ±256 or ±512 to a previously observed page. A previous-page handle excludes the current page. No virtual adjacency or future translation. These measurements bound this menu; an unseen physical page needs some other address-generation interface. The exact physical successor control can generate a target previously trained on this recording when its destination page is already observed here. Earliest opportunity scans consider the preceding 64 ingresses before the first next use, in cycles and events, separate from forecast accuracy.
5. Split each ROI by LLC arrival order into 60% training, 20% development, 20% evaluation. Do not let a label cross a split. Report development results without tuning rules, capacities, horizons or thresholds. The timely-opportunity proxy requires at least the median completed demand dispatch→fill time observed in the source training prefix; use an explicit 128-cycle fallback if no completed observations are available. Also report exact correctness, lead in cycles, dynamic event distances, and target outcomes separately. An additive gain is a unique original demand-miss ID for which the richer rule names the physical address with that lead, while the simpler rule does not. "Head-exposed" requires positive recorded operand retirement-head wait; never sum these waits into a time-saving estimate.
6. Find genuinely ambiguous local contexts. A qualifying example requires at least two distinct legal target actions under exactly the same local key in training, with two supported richer keys preferring different actions in training. Demand that the respective alternatives both recur on separate suffix targets and that at least one correct suffix choice reaches an original miss with useful lead. Report example IDs, PC, earlier cues, support, addresses, deadline, Stride and strong native first-use outcomes. The measured precision uses every offered target, including negative contexts. For transfer, pool only the training prefixes of discovery cases from *other* collection families; use keys with no raw PC or physical page ID. No transition truth, future target, future hit or criticality is an input.
7. Rank hypotheses using observed frequency, retained test coverage, utility beyond multi-delta and SPP, and head exposure. Group alternative projections of the same load by its original ID. Arrival-time feedback, cache pollution, MLP and actual performance changes after issuing hypothetical requests remain outside these offline tests. Native differences among the four controls are useful execution measurements, but do not estimate the causal effect of the offline forecasts.

After the first 250k-instruction screen, two follow-ups were registered. Apply the same thresholds, vocabulary, estimators and chronological splits to all seven Google discovery recordings at 2M skip, 1M warmup, 2M ROI. Analyze these separately in long_google; selection used first-interval support and included the entire Google discovery panel. For interpreting address successes at longer horizons, add conventional fixed distance h, same-PC stride times h, same-PC interarrival period times stride, and a single training-frequent signed displacement. Their outputs use the same physical legality checks and address deadlines. Among four-event windows where multiple PCs each sustain a repeated per-PC delta, use the most frequent training PC/delta motif as a descriptive test-window exemplar. Show trained motifs and missing support. These controls were motivated by the first screen's frequent physical-offset successes and sparse double-branch examples.

A final exploratory sensitivity asks whether limiting supervision to a specific ordinal future suppressed a signal. Using the already chosen trace panels, score membership in the next 1–16 demand ingresses with a single offer and the same mandatory service-reference lead. Include each new physical line only at its first subsequent use and fit all legal future lines, without selecting training targets by NoPF labels, timing outcome or head exposure. Compare a single training-frequent displacement, next-line, offset, local context, previous PC and ordered/unordered past PCs. Keep the 256-key limit. Separately diagnose exact-key support at the four primary horizons, including in an unrestricted training dictionary. These sensitivity tests were added after inspection of primary and long-window support. They carry no independent confirmatory status.
