# Which access relationships survive a change of context?

The Google discovery recordings contain recurring program structure that conventional address generators can exploit. In one Delta interval, two different load PCs each advanced by **two physical lines** and alternated, so the combined stream advanced **one**. In a different Charlie interval, knowing the preceding PC suggested a particular future line that four simpler offline rules missed. These findings have very different frequencies and timing margins. Only the four conventional prefetchers have native execution-time measurements in this study. All counted rules here are offline, frozen probes of the original Stride timing trajectory. ## Did earlier context generalize?

- Across **all seven Google primary suffixes and all seven deeper suffixes**, none of the fixed-horizon, exact-local-key tests produced both recurring, correctly distinguished alternatives with at least one timely-margin original miss. MCF supplied that evidence in the primary collection. The comparison requires a supported local key and two successful different actions under earlier context; a positive needs both futures in the test suffix. Sparse observations, the 256-key cap and the finite physical menu limit its power. In deep Arizona at distance 16, 3,974/3,999 query keys had never appeared in training; 24 lacked three observations and one had no legal training target. In deep Delta this unseen count was 2,799/2,925. [Primary ambiguity](ambiguity.csv) · [Deep ambiguity](long_google/ambiguity.csv) · [Support by exclusion](long_google/key_support.csv)
- Deep Google phase behavior changed sharply. The most frequent stable, mixed-PC four-event word in deep Delta appeared 672 times in its own training segment and zero times in its own suffix. At distance 16, offset-only and fixed +16 named 13/12 original targets with reference lead in this region, compared with 793/793 in the first. In deep Arizona those two counts tied at 1,128, and in deep Charlie they were 589/587. A deeper window can increase the number of observed accesses without restoring the same phase. [Deep mixed-PC windows](long_google/interleaved_motifs.csv) · [Deep offset and cadence](long_google/distance_controls.csv)
- Three probes discard raw PC and page IDs and train on the first 60% of all discovery **collections other than the target's collection**. Evaluation uses only the target's final 20% and a source-only service reference. At distance 16, transferred anonymous ordering reached 101 CLIP and 1,104 Llama original targets. Constant +16 in the within-record reference reached 112 and 1,233. Source-specific reference delays differ; this comparison identifies a simple explanation, not equal-deadline superiority. Early Delta's transferred anonymous-order rule reached zero while the withheld-collection offset-only rule reached 17. These are exploratory transfers, not a use of the independent reserved families. [Rows with fit=other_collections](scores.csv) · [Separate same-record cross-interval test](../transfer/)
- Data-derived query choices are held fixed during evaluation. Suffix “accuracy” is exact physical addresses matched per offer, and every offered address with no suitable query outcome counts against precision. No-offer anchors remain in full-stream denominators. Fixed-ordinal cases require the first subsequent use of the offered line at the specified event, with a 64-event guard for the same-PC task. The exploratory unordered window scores each target once for coverage even when multiple valid offers hit it. It counts matches to all legal future addresses before consulting NoPF outcomes. ## How to reproduce and interpret the files

From the workload expansion directory, after producing the four native histories per recording:

```sh
python3 -B patterns/discovery.py
python3 -B patterns/controls.py
python3 -B patterns/window.py
python3 -B patterns/verify.py
```

For the longer Google phase, run the first three commands with `--history-root raw/long_google/history --output patterns/long_google --case gg_arizona gg_charlie gg_delta gg_merced gg_tahoe gg_tango gg_yankee`. Run the last command with only the history-root and output options. Each case list must be a subset of the frozen discovery allowlist. NumPy is required. Both outputs passed fixture checks for prefix causality, split guards and legal physical construction; all exported fixed-horizon witness operands, alternatives, time gaps, labels and physical addresses were independently checked against native history.

[Candidate reach](candidate_reach.csv) asks how far before an original miss at least one eligible trigger could generate its exact physical line, given hindsight, from specified physical-page handles and offsets. [Added targets](added_targets.csv) asks which earlier cue predicts an actual target that its simpler control missed, with source-derived lead. [Context support](key_support.csv) counts unseen, low-support, unrepresentable training, capacity-excluded and represented keys. [Never-timely and interrupted streams](sequence_populations.csv) separately uses complete per-policy arrival sequences, per-PC and per-page order. “T” requires matched NoPF miss and a direct timely first use. “L” is a matched late transaction, “P” a paired tag miss without a late claim, and “H” an ordinary paired LLC hit. “Strong miss” requires an actual tag miss under both native multi-delta and native SPP on the same original demand operand. Reporting the most recently visited physical pages uses only past accesses; testing legal reach with future target IDs is retrospective. No diagnostic forecast was admitted into an LLC queue in these analyses.
