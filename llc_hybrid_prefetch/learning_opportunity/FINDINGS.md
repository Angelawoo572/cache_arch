# Which address relationships survive a held-out test?

Across eleven external SPEC and GAP recordings, adding 32 ordered global arrivals to same-PC and region history reduced the BFS-trained probe's held-out log loss on **all eleven**. The improvement survived restriction to targets in the training vocabulary on ten. At one proposed address per cue, the richer probe identified more exact, legal eighth targets with at least 130 cycles of lead on **four** recordings, tied on **four** and identified fewer on **three**. Across 66,147 sampled cues the net change was −80, including −84 on GCC-734B and +14 on Soplex-247B. More history helped assign probabilities to futures. Turning that signal into an additional useful address remains the harder test. [External comparisons](WORKLOADS.md) · [Underlying scores](probes/scores.csv)

## Information

The gains also have to occur where the conventional predictor needs help. Across those eleven test sets, adding global history gave a net **−15** qualified forecasts on 2,104 original misses where SPP was late, and **zero** on 12,195 persistent SPP misses. A balanced training pool of BFS, MCF and Soplex added **2,548** qualified forecasts on 9,334 original misses already timely under SPP. Its net changes were **−6** on the late cohort and **−5** on the persistent cohort. These are paired changes within each trained vocabulary. An aggregate of zero can include individual gains and losses. [Per-recording results and denominators](external_evidence.csv) · [Training-pool sensitivity](WORKLOADS.md)

**One real relationship did anticipate SPP.** On MCF17-1644B we applied the frozen BFS model to SPP's own history and joined each forecast to the request that actually claimed the load. Of 1,699 sampled original misses with a late SPP claim, global history identified 59 exact targets before SPP proposed them. The median advance was 1,034 cycles. For 57 targets the available lead exceeded the request's measured proposal-to-fill duration. Same-PC and region history identified four. All 53 gains unique to global history were four or five lines ahead of their cues, with 46 at five lines. When the models could abstain, global history identified two early targets and local history three. The signal involves choosing when to use a short displacement across PCs. The model seldom gave that choice its highest probability once abstention was available. [Same-clock analysis, actual requests and dynamic examples](probes/SPP_CLOCK.md)

A source-trained address probe can exploit context beyond the current PC on real traces. On the independent BFS-14 recording, a probe trained on BFS-3 predicted the exact eighth future line on a legally observed page with at least 130 cycles of lead at 1,765 of 6,001 cues. With the same-PC and region history alone it did so at 1,558 cues. Both probes made one proposal at every cue using the same training vocabulary. These counts measure address identification before request admission. Held-out log loss measures how well this fitted probe assigned probability to this vocabulary. Its difference across features is neither a calibrated information ceiling nor the mutual information in the raw trace. [Feature comparisons](probes/FINDINGS.md) · [Paired targets](probes/paired_targets.csv)

The 207-target net gain has an important qualification. Of the original misses SPP directly covered on time, the richer probe gained 217 targets and lost 21. Among 334 SPP-late original misses it gained six and lost three. Among 865 persistent SPP original misses it gained one and lost two. The probe's lead is measured in Stride. The SPP classification is joined by dynamic operand. These two clocks do not establish earlier issue in SPP. Most of the extra information recognized a part of the sequence where SPP already worked. A correct forecast of an SPP-timely load could still prove useful under a different resource budget, but this comparison supplies no evidence of that benefit. [Per-target overlap](probes/paired_targets.csv)

## Generalization

With Soplex as donor and BFS-3 as test, all 1,033 new legal, lead-qualified eighth-target hits over the abstaining same-PC probe were eight physical lines ahead. They spanned five cue PCs and 75 target regions. Under a forced single proposal per cue, the richer model found 1,091 qualifying targets versus 202 for same-PC history. This is automatic transfer of a relative-access relation. Its concentration on one displacement limits what it establishes about learning complex relations. [Target concentration and outcomes](probes/paired_targets.csv) · [Input and physical placement controls](probes/FINDINGS.md)

The same compiled BFS kernel on two named inputs provides a stricter input-transfer check. Only 145 of 7,079 Stanford-to-Google test targets lay in the donor's eighth-target displacement vocabulary. Both abstaining probes had zero legal, lead-qualified matches. Separately, the nonuniform MCF page-placement replay changed 11,181 of 12,778 physical frames while retaining all 608,699 source load operands. Even the number of Stride admissions changed, from 22,294 to 22,290. Matching by LLC rank across placements would have compared different dynamic loads. [Named input transfer](probes/FINDINGS.md) · [Verified physical mappings](capture/placement.csv)

The frozen BFS probe also faced two different workloads. On Llama2-stories15M, the training vocabulary could name 5,973 of 6,014 eighth targets. At one address per cue the local-plus-region probe recovered 5,228 legal targets with sufficient lead. Adding 32 globally ordered events reduced that to 5,138. On Google Charlie the vocabulary could name 1,570 of 6,063 targets and the corresponding score fell from 318 to 301. ## Physical reach and deadlines

In MCF-472B at horizon eight, 3,152 of 3,384 eligible pairs named a line absent from the measured LLC prefix on an already observed physical page. A second MCF recording, 1644B, had 8,789 such pairs among 8,921 eligible; the median legal choice set for reachable novel targets grew from 128 lines to 640. At Soplex-92B the novel count was 574 of 4,438. Each pair required an original NoPF miss, a plain miss or late claim under all three conventional policies, a target page observed within the bounded history, and a measured lead of at least 130 cycles. These observations show how the address-generation problem differs between programs. The target was revealed only to the label query. A line classified as new here could have appeared during warmup or before the recording. [Reach denominators and vocabulary sizes](labels/RESULTS.md)

On MCF-472B, Stride had 539 late original first uses among 8,192 sampled admissions. Their median proposal-to-demand-admission interval was two cycles. Median request-queue delay was 20 cycles and median dispatch-to-fill time was 95 cycles. For 531 targets a same-page observation in the preceding 64 admissions predated the actual proposal. For 475 there was enough time at the earliest such observation to cover the observed request service duration. This is a bounded retrospective timing test. It identifies promising deadlines without attributing predictive information to the earlier cue. [Sampled service distributions](resources/request_service.csv) · [Request-level examples](resources/request_examples.csv)

PageRank showed a different limit. Of 32,768 sampled eighth-arrival targets, 12,291 were original misses that remained late or missed under all three conventional controls. Only 1,055 lay on a page seen in the last 64 arrivals and had at least 130 cycles of lead. Across all sampled targets, every one of the 3,590 that met the recent-page condition already had at least 256 cycles of lead. Selecting a legal target page is the more immediate problem in this sample. [Page and deadline counts](labels/results/pr_heldout__stride_horizons.csv)

## How to interpret performance

On MCF-1644B we replayed the first diagnosed service-qualified late-target example. The probe's line 38,740,853 replaced SPP's offer of line 38,740,850 at the same cue. The target was an original NoPF miss, merged late under SPP and became a directly attributed timely first use in replay. Across the full 200M instructions, the changed run saved 9,724 cycles (0.00074%) and moved 14,400 fewer DRAM bytes. It gained 31,590 original timely first uses and lost 31,754. The address was generated causally. Choosing this successful forecast for replay used the observed target and SPP lateness, so it demonstrates a realizable individual conversion and supplies no estimate of unconditional hybrid speedup. [Matched demand and actual fill](trials/README.md) · [Whole-run counts](trials/forecast_effect.csv)

A prospective held-out BFS forecast selected without demand outcomes generated physical line 402,478, exactly the eighth later LLC arrival, with 26,157 cycles of lead. Real replay accepted the offer and the intended load consumed it on time. That same load already hit in NoPF and consumed a timely prefetch in Stride. Across the 200M interval, replacing one Stride offer gained nine and lost thirteen timely first uses of original misses, reduced DRAM traffic by 64 bytes and finished 784 cycles later. One tested decision cannot estimate an online hybrid's average effect. [Prospective forecast replay](one_offer/README.md)

In a separate diagnostic, SPP received the same 18 retrospectively known same-page targets in two timing treatments. Early offers made 18/18 timely across MCF, BFS and Soplex. Near-demand offers made 0/18 timely. The early treatment saved 148,219 cycles on MCF and 106,164 on BFS, but cost 392,638 cycles on Soplex. Each workload retired 200M instructions. Changed decisions displaced other useful requests, and the sign of the time change depended on the workload. [Counterfactual effects and raw timing](trials/README.md)

The original-miss denominator is a dynamic NoPF operand. A direct timely first use requires the matching operand to consume a prefetched line. An ordinary hit, private-cache merge or change in LLC visibility receives a separate classification. IPC and elapsed cycles use identical retired work. The raw record for every prefetch policy retains its own clock, ingress sequence and page map. ## What the evidence supports

| Question | Finding | Smallest next test and hardware cost to measure |
|---|---|---|
| Can earlier context identify an address before a strong conventional predictor? **Supported on MCF-1644B.** | Ordered history identified the short cross-PC displacements above before SPP's actual proposals. Confidence determined whether the probe issued them. | Freeze a decision threshold on separate development data and give global and local inputs equal offers per thousand arrivals. Count lead, first uses and displaced requests. A line add is cheap. Supplying ordered PC, delta and time history costs storage, read ports and decision latency. |
| Can a predictor generalize to an exact line it has never seen? **Reach is supported. Reliable identification remains open.** | Two MCF recordings had many underserved new lines on known pages. A change of BFS graph input left few eighth targets in the donor's displacement alphabet. | Freeze address construction on one recording and score unseen lines on known pages under a second recording and nonuniform placement. Compare page-tag storage, candidate count and the cost of selecting one offset before the deadline. |
| Does extra history consistently complement conventional coverage? **Unsupported for the tested probes and budgets.** | Across eleven external programs or recordings, global history lowered log loss. Most of the large pooled gain was already timely under SPP. | Retain all cues, including streams with zero timely first uses. Score new original-miss coverage under identical admission rules, bandwidth and inference delay. Measure history access energy and decisions per cycle at the observed burst rate. |
| Would converting additional misses into first uses shorten execution? **Workload and decision dependent.** | Moving the same known targets earlier saved cycles in two of three full replays. The prospectively chosen BFS address was correct and timely, and the execution still took 784 more cycles. | Replay a frozen stream of predictions under contention with a common issuer. Measure each core's finish time, memory traffic and the timely requests lost to displacement. |

For scale, 32 history entries containing a 16-bit PC tag, 12-bit signed delta and 8-bit age would occupy 144 bytes before access ports, validity and update logic. Eight 40-bit physical page tags would occupy another 40 bytes before recency metadata. These are illustrative field budgets. The fitted software probes use richer values, and their measured accuracy does not carry over to a reduced encoding without a new test.

These tests can use single addresses, multiple future targets or other causal output representations supported by the indexed labels. The study has measured no neural circuit area, energy, initiation interval or end-to-end hybrid speedup.
