# Does changing the prefetch distance protect the first miss after a timely run?

On the MCF+CactuBSSN mix, always looking two strides ahead made **30 of 35** original first late failures timely. It preserved the immediately preceding timely load in 29 of those repairs, and the *entire* original timely prefix in 17. The same policy accepted 473 fewer requests and saved 92,273 mix cycles. On the same recording, anticipating a previously observed run ending made **12 of 36** first persistent failures timely. **None** of those 12 kept the immediately preceding timely hit. The latter policy cost 228,234 mix cycles. Repairing a formerly missed address can simply move the break in coverage. [Cohort outcomes](cohorts.csv) · [Fixed-work performance](performance.csv)

## What was tested

- The native NoPF/Stride pair supplies the reference roster. A timely load here is an original demand LLC miss that Stride converted into a hit with verified first-use attribution. After at least two such loads, the next original miss is classified as a first late failure (L) when the demand merges with a Stride prefetch, or another first persistent failure (P). The endpoints, original timely prefix and following recovery are fixed *before* evaluating a changed policy. Recoveries can be right-censored at the end of the recording. [Episode definitions and dynamic identities](../episodes/)
- The primary comparison uses per-core LLC ingress order. Seven additional views capture instruction/operand order and corresponding PC-, page- and four-page-local projections. A load can occur in several views. [Unique original failure identities](unique_targets.csv) deduplicates across views per core and policy. Prefix preservation counts individual loads and entire prefixes separately. A changed load can have a directly timely use, a late merge, a confirmed ordinary LLC hit, an unclaimed miss, or no independent LLC lookup. A blank recovery distance means there was no later attributable timely demand in that projection. A zero in a cohort with zero episodes contributes no evidence.
- The baseline, unconditional lookahead-2, paced-distance and repeated-run-ending policies use the same one-candidate-per-admission issuer. A 64-row tagged PC table extends a native stride by a second step only after four equal observed deltas, at most 128 cycles between the last two same-PC arrivals, and LLC/DRAM occupancy below the registered thresholds. A zero delta interrupts the local run. The turn variant also remembers the length, regular stride and departure jump of a run seen twice at that PC, and attempts its next region up to two local steps early. Both new mechanisms stay on the current physical page. These thresholds were fixed before reading the episode roster. [Registered protocol](protocol.md) · [Online decision logic](rules.hh)
- The processor replays complete fixed-work intervals, including all unselected demands and shared-resource feedback. An earlier fill on one target can change later issue decisions. The modified policies never observe the label identifying the last timely access or next failure. All 12 native controls reproduced the archived core cycles and system statistics exactly. A separate implementation reproduced every choice over **6,662,177 warmup and measured ingresses** across 48 primary arms. A future-suffix perturbation test also checks the compiled rule's common prefix. [Checks and counts](validation.csv)

## What was preserved and what it cost

| Recording, original failure | Policy | Converted / original failures | Converted with predecessor intact | Converted with whole prefix intact | Whole-run cycles saved |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MCF in MCF+CactuBSSN, L | Lookahead-2 | 30 / 35 | 29 | 17 | 92,273 |
| MCF in MCF+CactuBSSN, P | Repeated-turn | 12 / 36 | 0 | 0 | −228,234 |
| BFS-3 later, P | Lookahead-2 | 445 / 2,700 | 344 | 208 | −85,677 |
| BFS-8 alone, L | Lookahead-2 | 516 / 516 | 516 | 295 | −4,141 |
| BFS-8 with LBM, L | Lookahead-2 | 2,586 / 2,742 | 2,483 | 2,047 | 478,762 |
| Alternating 8/24-line rows, P | Repeated-turn | 0 / 511 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- When BFS-8 runs with LBM, the fixed lookahead protects many first late failures, at a cost of 62,592 extra DRAM bytes. BFS-8 finishes 478,762 cycles earlier and its LBM peer 77,539 cycles later. The paced rule converts only 647/2,742 and the mix takes 166,956 more cycles than native. Alone, BFS-8's fixed lookahead converts every one of its 516 first late failures, yet 221 original prefix loads lose timely attribution and execution takes 4,141 additional cycles. These policies run on every eligible anchor. The makespan differences apply to the complete policies.
- Observed recovery can move independently of the first miss. For the 35 first late MCF failures, lookahead-2 preserves the original recovery demand as timely in 24 cases. On alternating two-length rows, pushing requests two lines ahead leaves all 511 first failures unresolved, removes one timely prefix load per episode and shifts the median next-timely distance from three to four original ingresses. The repeated-turn rule requires two matching completed run signatures, so alternating lengths defeated its confidence test. The changed policy's recovery distance counts original positions in the same projected stream, including newly timely loads. It is conditional on observing recovery.
- MCF load 10,138,311 illustrates a narrow deadline. Unconditional lookahead at instruction 10,138,275 issued line 370557 at cycle 10,496,773, dispatched at 10,496,793, and filled at 10,496,876. Its demand entered two cycles after fill, and hit at 10,496,898. The preceding last-timely load survived, while the other member of the two-load prefix lost timely attribution. Both runs recorded zero retirement-head wait for this target. MCF load 10,105,957 shows the complementary failure. The turn rule predicted at instruction 10,105,888 and filled at 10,296,544, before the target's ingress at 10,296,759. Both members of its previous timely prefix lost attribution. [Per-demand timelines](examples.csv)
- On MCF+CactuBSSN, lookahead-2 turned 3,110 native MCF hits into misses; 172 had a prefetch fill as the last observed evictor of that line. On CactuBSSN, 8 of 23 reversals had an MCF prefetch as last evictor. With repeated-turn, 119 of 4,412 MCF reversals had that association. These are temporal displacement associations. No additive isolated execution cost is assigned to each eviction. Some lost hits reflect lines whose original helpful prefetch was never issued. [Paired victim evidence](pollution.csv)
- The compact run-length mechanism and timing gate produced no positive claim of a general solution across the registered panel. Solo MCF region 2 and the three-stream switched-PC-control stimulus with a shared PC have no qualifying primary global-ingress episodes. Private-cache filtering, interleaving and observation order limit which windows offer evidence. [All 12 cases, including zero denominators](cohorts.csv)

### A fixed current cue transfers in LBM

- The R1-trained current-context policy takes **7,293,642** cycles for 5M measured R2 instructions. Native Stride takes 7,563,339. Adding the previous offset on the page takes 7,300,093, 6,451 cycles longer than the current-context arm. The alternate older cue takes 7,297,705, and the previous global PC takes 7,298,295. Training-only constant +5 takes 7,387,392, while +1 takes 7,623,711. Four shadows reproduced all native statistics and every original per-grant decision. All four representations made offers at all 3,447 slots on the unchanged reference stream. [Whole-run and per-core results](lbm_performance.csv)
- On 4,020 original first persistent global-ingress endpoints, the page rule produces 2,141 timely first uses (1,779 with the original prefix intact); the current-context rule produces 1,792 (1,435 with the prefix intact). For 1,543 of the current-context arm’s 1,792 conversions the winning prediction was issued strictly before the original last-timely operand entered the LLC on this arm. The other 249 were issued on that ingress cycle. The latter policy also loses timely attribution on 1,842 of 12,123 originally timely demands across the full ROI and transfers precisely the native 12,368,384 DRAM bytes. The page rule loses 1,777 originally timely demands and transfers 128 fewer bytes than native. The extra first failures repaired by the page rule do not translate into a win over current context in these fixed-work runs. [Fixed endpoints](lbm_cohorts.csv)
- R1 contained 214 guard-qualified ingresses at full PC 4,201,648 and current-page offset 58. In 59 of those cases the designated future first-failure target was at offset 63 and satisfied the training lead constraint. At one such R1 occurrence, instructions 10,015,601 and 10,015,691 supplied the cue and the later demand. In R2 this same PC and offset recur at instruction 12,656,273, physical line 552058. The current-context policy proposes line 552063, five lines ahead, before the original last timely instruction 12,656,771. The request is accepted at 7,792,445, dispatches at 7,792,465, fills at 7,792,677 and target 12,657,217 hits at 7,793,646. The target's head wait falls from 333 to 10 cycles. Constant +5 also covers this particular occurrence; +1 misses. These are measured cycle indices within each arm. [Training occurrence and target timelines across all arms](lbm_transfer_witness.csv)
- At this **same PC**, offset 11 instead predicts offset 21, ten lines ahead (54 qualifying targets in 214 R1 observations). In R2 the cue is 10,397,889 on line 451147, before original last-timely 10,399,331. Target 10,399,777, line 451157, misses native, +1 and +5, with head waits of 586, 417 and 572 cycles respectively. The transferred current-context request is accepted at 4,396,910, dispatched at 4,396,930 and filled at 4,397,145. The demand enters at 4,398,981 and hits at 4,399,001 with 13 head-wait cycles. The +5 and +10 examples therefore use different displacements at the same PC, with physical-page offset separating them on both recordings. The examples were selected after scoring for repeated training support and lowered observed target head wait. [Per-arm evidence](lbm_transfer_witness.csv)
- The strongest selected occurrence starts at **offset 15 and targets offset 22** at the same PC (54 qualifying targets in 214 R1 observations). In R2, instruction 14,971,705 proposes physical line 650838 from 650831. Both members of the original two-hit prefix, ending at 14,972,675, remain directly timely. The original first persistent miss 14,973,121 becomes an attributable timely hit. The current-context request has 2,075 cycles of proposal-to-demand lead and fills 1,766 cycles before ingress; target head wait falls from 261 to 39. SPP also predicts the line, but waits until the last timely instruction, leaving 267 cycles of lead; its fill is 98 cycles after ingress. Multidelta has 343 cycles of lead and fills 22 cycles late. Cross-PC and temporal have no prior proposal for that line since its preceding LLC ingress. Across these 4,020 primary targets the current rule makes 1,792 timely and 73 of those lack an attributable timely use in *any* of the four open controls. This occurrence was chosen among the 43 that also met the audit's training support, earlier-cue, bounded lead and head-improvement conditions. The two preceding examples are already covered by full-rate SPP. These individual differences do not establish a whole-run advantage over SPP. [Cross-arm chronology](lbm_witness_controls.csv) · [Witness selection counts](lbm_witness_selection.csv) · [Strong-control whole-window outcomes](../strong_controls/results.csv)
- “Current context” uses low 12 bits of PC and the exact six-bit line offset with a common local-delta fallback. Its key is hashed. Among 554 distinct raw pairs in the guarded R1 fit, 406 hash buckets occur, including 116 buckets that represent more than one pair. The illustrated 58→63 training context occupies a bucket with just one raw pair. This finite recording supports a recurring offset-conditioned address; no symbol or source loop was established.

## Reproduction

Run from the LLC project root. The baseline traces and canonical NoPF/Stride runs come from the parent studies. Generate the fixed episode roster (including supplementary LBM R2), the per-core arrays with the episode study, and the archived train/test arrays for the predictability study first. The table-preparation command refits BFS-3 and the two-length row workload and creates all four LBM transfer tables with their shadow reference CSVs. Python scripts using NumPy require that package. Raw logs and build outputs remain under the transition study's raw directory.

```sh
python3 transition_study/replay/build.py
python3 transition_study/replay/run.py --jobs 4
python3 transition_study/replay/check_rules.py --jobs 4
python3 transition_study/replay/score.py --jobs 4
python3 transition_study/replay/pollution.py
python3 transition_study/replay/prepare_tables.py
python3 transition_study/replay/supplement.py
python3 transition_study/replay/lbm.py
python3 transition_study/strong_controls/run.py
python3 transition_study/replay/lbm_witness.py
python3 transition_study/replay/witness_controls.py
python3 transition_study/replay/check_results.py
```
