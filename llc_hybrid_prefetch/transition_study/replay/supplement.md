# Supplementary frozen-table comparison

Registered after the offline held-out scores were known and before the native runs below. The preregistered 48 full-window comparisons retain their separate controls. This supplement uses only later BFS-3 and the alternating two-length rows on their original placement.

- Freeze the frequency tables, action vocabulary, default scores and data-independent one-in-eight grants exported by the predictability study. Use its fixed held-out ingress-index bounds and train on its designated prefix only. The runtime engine sees current requesting PC, physical line, cycle and its own previous observations, including earlier ROI events before grants open.
- Compare three replays on both recordings. A shadow computes candidate choices without modifying native Stride. A matched next-line control offers the next same-page line on the same slots where the engine offers a legal candidate. The table arm offers its own frozen address. For this comparison a candidate crossing the current physical page is declined in both arms; both retain ordinary native Stride on all other opportunities. A valid auxiliary choice identical to native keeps native attribution. A different legal choice replaces that anchor's native choice. Eligibility checks, physical ownership, shared queues, and degree one apply in every arm. If a proposed auxiliary is rejected by the queue there is no second issue attempt. Different live executions can diverge, and matched grants on the unchanged reference do not guarantee equal bytes or accepted counts.
- Compare full ROI cycles, accepted/claimed counts, DRAM bytes, preservation of stable-timely prefixes and conversions of the previously defined first late and persistent targets. Report raw targets already observed in the offline study separately from actual native effects. The frozen counts use long-lived reference state and ideal computation timing; this tests the information content, not a hardware implementation. No threshold or table is tuned after replay.
