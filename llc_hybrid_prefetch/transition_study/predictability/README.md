# Can an earlier LLC access name the first miss after a run of useful prefetches?

In LBM, a rule fitted on one recording named 1,926 of 4,020 transition targets in the other recording at one fixed opportunity per eight LLC ingresses. An earlier-offset alternative reached exactly the same 1,926 dynamic loads. Conditioning on the immediately preceding global PC reached 1,949. In two newly held-out BFS intervals, simple next-line outperformed the frozen history rule. The causal cues exist, but how to represent them depends on which stream and which transition the hardware can observe.

## Trigger, target and deadline

Each measured demand ingress on the selected core is a possible trigger, including hits, misses and long periods with no upcoming transition. Candidate generation sees the current load PC and physical line plus past ingresses. Retrospective labels identify a run of at least two successive useful prefetch hits followed immediately by a late or persistent demand miss. A useful hit must be an original NoPF miss, a native Stride hit, and a timely first use credited to a prefetch. A late endpoint is a paired demand miss with attributable late use. Other paired misses are called persistent here. An unmatched or other event breaks the run. The main view uses core arrival order. The by-PC view applies the identical definition and forecast to successive ingresses of the same load PC. We retain intervening accesses by other PCs for the physical time and event bounds. Original instruction and operand IDs identify a load across runs. [Reconciliation with independently extracted episode IDs](cohort_checks.csv)

At each trigger, the supervised target is the **first future endpoint in the selected stream**. Four independent fits use ranges of 1–4, 5–16, 17–64 and 1–64 physical demand ingresses, with a 4,096-cycle limit. A correct choice must target the exact physical line at its first subsequent use, with enough lead to meet the source training prefix's median observed primary demand dispatch-to-fill latency. In the primary pooled window we also report address matches that fell short of that reference. The service reference can differ from the realized service cost. We compare against actual native proposals for that physical line after its previous demand and before the target, including subsequently rejected proposals. Absence is relative to the measured trace. The scores are opportunities on the original timing trajectory. [Full horizon, timing and false-proposal counts](scores.csv)

The causal output menu contains all other offsets on the current physical page and 12 signed displacements to previously observed physical pages, ±16, 32, 64, 128, 256 or 512 lines. For cross-page displacements the original reference's physical page must already have been observed by that core. An unseen page cannot be inferred from a virtual neighbor. Simple next-line and per-PC stride controls stay on the current page. The evaluated association emits at most one physical candidate.

## Baselines and blinding

- Each source fits on its first 60% of ingresses. The following 20% calibrates an optional confidence gate. The last 20% supplies within-record tests. Transfer tests freeze the source and score the entire separate recording, initializing diagnostic history from its first measured ingress. We discard decision points whose event or time horizon could cross the right split boundary. Observed training fills used to select the deadline must complete before the training cutoff.
- The fixed-grant comparison gives every mechanism the same data-independent slot per eight ingresses. The other gates score every legal trigger and optionally apply a source-development confidence threshold. The threshold is the first prespecified cutoff whose validation offer rate is at most 1/8. Target-side drift can produce far fewer offers. Results list both eligible triggers and actual choices.
- Fitted tables estimate the probability that a query has its next endpoint at a specified physical action, including negative anchors in the denominator. A context needs eight training observations; eight parent pseudocounts smooth its estimates. Unseen or sparse contexts use their declared parent. PC-local delta, two-event PC/delta history, normalized PC bits, exact current offset, earlier offset, and a completed native timely-hit streak have separate controls. The page rule uses the last offset previously observed on this physical page within 64 ingresses and 4,096 cycles. Its fallback uses low PC bits, an eight-line offset group and the last same-PC delta class. First-use provenance or tag feedback may enter the feedback ablation only after tag completion. No model receives a last-hit flag, a future miss PC, NoPF outcomes, or the eventual run length at inference. [Ablations](matched_controls.csv) · [Address and target overlap](control_overlap.csv)

## False proposals and scarce evidence

On LBM transfer, 2,261 of 3,447 page choices matched a timely endpoint; multiple grants can target the same load. Seven of the original 115 stable continuations received a grant and all seven choices missed the timely endpoint objective. On the BFS-3-late suffix, 14 of 1,460 page choices matched, with 17 false choices on 17 granted stable continuations (127 such continuations surrounded eligible triggers). On the two-row → five-row transfer, 269 of 986 offers matched; 499 of 695 offers on 5,586 stable continuations missed the endpoint objective. A “false” choice here may still prefetch an ordinary demand and consume resources. These counts do not measure cache pollution.

Without grant throttling, 124 of 127 stable BFS choices and 115 of 115 stable LBM choices missed the endpoint objective. The dev-calibrated BFS gate issued 131 times and reached three endpoints. In LBM the same calibration procedure issued zero times on the new recording. Per-rule scores expose both completely unseen contexts and contexts with at least eight training observations. The held-out BFS-8 midpoint had 49,891 unseen page keys at 49,891 evaluated ingresses under the early BFS-3 fit. Table support and temporal coverage matter when interpreting a sparse positive. ## What happened when those choices were really issued?

On LBM R2 the measured Stride baseline took 7,563,339 cycles. The R1-fitted page rule saved 263,246 cycles (3.481%). Exact current PC/offset saved **269,697 cycles (3.566%)**, despite reaching fewer targets of the offline episode label. The shifted older index saved 265,634; previous global PC saved 265,044. Fixed +5 saved 175,947 and fixed +1 cost 60,372 cycles. Each of the four table-based native shadow arms reproduced all 3,447 reference decisions, the original timing and the original aggregate counters. These are fixed-work changes with real arbitration, self-induced timing and cache effects. [LBM full-window counters](../replay/lbm_performance.csv) · [LBM replay protocol](../replay/lbm_protocol.md)

On the BFS-3-late recording the frozen boundary rule saved 8,947 cycles across the whole interval, compared with 55,552 for equal-grant next-line. On the two-row synthetic recording the frozen rule **added 8,830 cycles**, while next-line saved 1,120. Issuing an early, correct address can displace a native choice that would have saved more time. ## Reproduction

From this directory with Python and NumPy, run `python3 predict.py --jobs 4`, `python3 predict.py --mid --jobs 2`, `python3 controls.py` `python3 deadlines.py` and `python3 verify.py`. The verification compiles a separate C++14 inference implementation, checks its actions and abstentions on full original trajectories, perturbs suffix PCs and physical lines to verify unchanged causal feature prefixes, and reconciles original instruction/operand pairs against the independently extracted episodes. The LBM exporter accepts `python3 export.py lbm-r1 lbm-r2 TABLE --mode page --expected REFERENCE.csv`. Available fixed representations are `page`, `exact`, `unrelated_older` and `last_global_pc`.
