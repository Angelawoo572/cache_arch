# What should machine learning contribute to a hybrid LLC prefetcher?

**Access prediction.** Can machine learning exploit temporal, spatial and cross-instruction correlations to anticipate a useful physical cache line when a conventional prefetcher would issue an inaccurate request, stay silent or name that line too late?

**Processor outcome.** Can a learned predictor cooperate with a low-cost conventional fast path so that more original demand misses become timely first-use hits and the same programs finish sooner, within realistic inference, bandwidth, metadata and cache-capacity budgets?

A prediction at cycle *t* is an action: generate a legal target address, or a small set of candidates with an issue order, from observations available by *t*. Its outputs could include an issue distance, urgency or confidence if those change the action. A baseline miss, a later load's address, a last timely hit, a later queue delay and retrospective retirement-head exposure can define analysis cohorts or training labels. An online predictor can consume past outcomes only after they become visible.

## Two timelines for one dynamic load

Align retirement work and the original core/instruction/operand, verifying the load PC and virtual cache line. Within each execution follow physical line, trigger, issue, dispatch, fill and first demand. Alignment supplies the answer to “what changed for this load?” Each execution's own clock supplies the answer to “how much time was available?” Page placement, request merging and private-cache filtering matter on both timelines. For each observed break compare the surrounding stream with a continuation that shares as much prior context as practicable. Ask which PC, delta, page or relative timing changes were visible *before the last successful hit*, which appeared only at the miss, and which could generate an address with useful lead. Include the false predictions made when a similar-looking stream kept succeeding. Score the entire eligible stream and preserve timely hits, ordinary residency and online service effects in replay. Reconfirmation can describe overlap between predictors; an outstanding physical request can only be issued once.

## Three independent hypotheses

| Capability | Proposed representation-independent test | What would undermine an ML explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Conditional pattern modeling** | At equal physical reach and grant rate, distinguish two futures with the same current stride and different earlier contexts. Test same-PC, cross-PC and interleaved-stream cues; use chronological and masked or shuffled controls. Count correct addresses and false proposals at continuing runs. | A run counter, stream separation, delta table, SPP or simple page-local association retains the same coverage and timing at comparable cost. |
| **B. Predictive lookahead** | Find the same future target at an earlier legal trigger. Compare its issue time with the conventional proposal on the same reference timeline. Separate proposal delay, queue delay and dispatch-to-fill delay; replay the change and observe previously timely requests too. | Extra lead from fixed distance or simple resource counters already yields the same improvement. Earlier actions increase pressure, displace useful requests, or fail to advance completion. |
| **C. Generalization across contexts** | Freeze the candidate generator and scoring rule, test a subsequent region or independent recording with unfamiliar PCs/pages, and compare with a global-frequency fallback and address-relative controls. Publish training support and unseen-PC rates. | Most correct targets come from a model-independent prior, next-line, or a phase already represented by an inexpensive table. Absence of usable cues at LLC ingress sets a separate limit. |

These capabilities can coexist. A conditional transition may justify a small MLP, table or convolution; long variable-distance relations might favor recurrence or content-based retrieval; a phase-dependent lead time might require only counters. A bounded record store, selective write gate, attention, agreement or criticality scoring becomes a design hypothesis after measurements identify the missing information and its deadline.
