# 605 development experiments

- Fit from NoPF at 10M, 30M, and 50M and used 70M and 130M for development. The input was the L2D miss stream entering the shared LLC. The fixed-pool experiments scored up to 16 candidates per trigger. Offline reach counts distinct NoPF LLC misses with a legal candidate and enough lead. The timing proxy weights each distinct target by the best estimated chance of filling before demand.

## Candidate reach

- **Oracle replay.** Match one candidate per trigger and each future NoPF miss at most once, using 280–4,000 cycles of lead. The physical frame changed for 129,122 of 149,930 aligned 10M demands. Keying the plan by core, instruction, operand, and virtual target line raised candidate matches from 1,860 to 12,501 for the same 13,218 planned targets. The oracle takes future NoPF outcomes as input. Plans are in `lstm/runs/v4/oracle/`. Replay logs are in `conventional/runs/v4_comparison/` under `oracle_v2_10m_d1/` and `oracle_virtual_v2_*/`. [Plan generator](v4_oracle_plan.py)
- **Candidate rotation.** Give priority to lines absent from each core’s 128-entry, 640-cycle proposal history. Keep the cap at 16. Across 10M, 30M, 50M, 70M, and 130M, reachable NoPF misses rose from 13,222 / 3,906 / 20,772 / 1,727 / 1,856 to 16,808 / 5,075 / 28,491 / 1,860 / 2,049. The rotated virtual oracle had lower IPC in four of five replays. It gained slightly at 50M, from 0.2160 to 0.2164, where native SPP reached 0.2185. Existing frozen weights also ranked the rotated pool worse at fixed decision budgets. See `lstm/runs/v4/reach_rotation_dev.csv`, `lstm/runs/v4/eval_rotation_frozen.csv`, and `conventional/runs/v4_comparison/oracle_virtual_rotate_*/summary.csv`. [Candidate probe](v4_data.py)

## Labels and timing

- **ROB criticality.** Log cycles with an in-flight load on the retirement-head instruction in NoPF. Join waits by core, instruction, and operand. The positive target waits had a 244-cycle p90. Test normalized wait as a multiplier on the calibrated soft label and as a weight for the same-source, opposite-offset ranking loss at temperatures 1.0 and 0.1. In the ROB-soft replays at 70M / 130M, LSTM IPC was 0.5810 / 0.6838 and event-only IPC was 0.5822 / 0.6858. The best native IPC was 0.5855 / 0.6900. Corrected wait logs are in `lstm/runs/v4/criticality/`, training results in `lstm/runs/v4/experiments/local_rob_*/`, and replays in `conventional/runs/v4_comparison/rob_{event,lstm}_*m/`. [Instrumentation](v4_criticality.py) · [Training](v4_train.py)
- **Timing features.** On 369,307 training NoPF demand services, the p90 of ingress-to-fill plus 16 modeled cycles was 245 / 365 / 429 cycles for ingress MSHR occupancy below 4 / 4–7 / 8–15. With the same 16 candidates, categorical PCs, recent per-core and shared arrival gaps, and ingress occupancy raised the event-only timing proxy at 4,000 decisions in both seeds. Seed 606 went from 62.0 / 312.2 to 735.6 / 713.9 expected timely unique targets at 70M / 130M. See `lstm/runs/v4/timing_probe_{association,prediction}.csv`. [Timing experiment](v5_timing.py)
- **Recurrent timing.** With the same event checkpoint as initialization, the eight-unit LSTM reached 795.1 / 766.1 expected timely unique targets at 4,000 decisions. The matched event-only fine-tune reached 778.5 / 754.4. Zeroing the LSTM’s hidden state reduced the proxy to 764.0 / 739.9. An ordered eight-event window scored 785.8 / 758.9 and beat both shuffled controls. These counts come from NoPF scoring. Checkpoints and logs are in `lstm/runs/v5_timing/{warm_event,warm_h8w32}/`. The window results are in `warm_h8w32/state/state_analysis.csv` under the same run root.
