\section*{Metrics: formula first}
Let $D=404{,}219$ be post-warmup demand L2 loads, $M$ the method's demand L2 misses, $R$ replayed prefetch-request events, $U$ useful-prefetch demand hits, $L$ demand misses merged into an in-flight prefetch, and $M_0=203{,}131$ no-prefetch L2 demand misses.
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{MR}_{L2}=100\frac{M}{D}}
\qquad
\boxed{A_{\mathrm{sel}}^{*}=100\frac{U}{R}}
\qquad
\boxed{C=100\frac{U}{M_0}}
\qquad
\boxed{T=100\frac{U}{U+L}}
\]
Because $D$ is identical across these matched rows, miss rate and miss reduction contain the same ordering information:
\[
\mathrm{MissReduction}=100\frac{M_0-M}{M_0},
\qquad \mathrm{MR}_{L2,0}=100\frac{M_0}{D}=50.2527\%.
\]
For an NN point $x$ and its matched offline normal baseline $N$, convert every component to a capped higher-is-better parity ratio:
\[
q_M=\min\!\left(1,\frac{\mathrm{MR}_{L2,N}}{\mathrm{MR}_{L2,x}}\right),\quad
q_A=\min\!\left(1,\frac{A^{*}_{\mathrm{sel},x}}{A^{*}_{\mathrm{sel},N}}\right),\quad
q_C=\min\!\left(1,\frac{C_x}{C_N}\right),\quad
q_T=\min\!\left(1,\frac{T_x}{T_N}\right).
\]
Miss rate and coverage are related cache outcomes, so they first form one block rather than receiving two full weights:
\[
\boxed{Q_{\mathrm{cache}}=\sqrt{q_Mq_C}},\qquad
\boxed{\mathrm{BPI}=100\left(Q_{\mathrm{cache}}q_Aq_T\right)^{1/3}}.
\]
The offline normal baseline is 100. Capping prevents an improvement in one component from hiding a deficit in another. BPI compares NN parity only against its corresponding normal policy; it does not replace IPC or rank different normal-prefetcher families.

\section*{Data $\rightarrow$ insight map}
\begin{center}\scriptsize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.23\textwidth}p{0.35\textwidth}X@{}}
\toprule
Question & Direct evidence & Supported insight \\
\midrule
Why does h16 beat stride? & IPC rises 0.00084, but BPI is only 73.24: miss rate, coverage, selected accuracy, and timeliness all lose parity. & The crossing is \textbf{not a balanced cache-side improvement}. Fewer requests may reduce bandwidth/pollution, or the remaining misses may be less critical, but those mechanisms are not yet counted.\\
Which point best preserves stride's balance? & h8 has the highest NN BPI (87.34), driven by 94.84\% selected-accuracy parity but only 54.04\% coverage parity. & h8 is the closest cache-side policy imitation even though h16 has the best IPC. BPI and IPC answer different questions.\\
Is h16 a saturation point? & h16 and h32 cross; h64 and h128 do not. Parameter count grows from 1,729 to 85,505 without monotonic IPC gain. & h16 is the first measured crossing, not a capacity saturation law. Width alone does not control outcome.\\
Does h128 look timely? & $T=99.977\%$ exceeds stride, but coverage is only 8.39\% and BPI is 83.77. & High timeliness can coexist with poor coverage when the gate suppresses opportunities. Capping $q_T$ prevents that surplus from hiding the deficit.\\
Did stateful TBPTT matter? & Corrected h16 rises from the pre-fix 0.37090 to 0.37100; h32 rises from 0.37033 to 0.37063. & Correct recurrence slightly strengthens the measured crossing, but the qualitative non-monotonic result remains.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\section*{Evidence boundary and next counters}
\begin{center}\scriptsize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lXX@{}}

Claim type & Supported now & Still required \\
\midrule
Performance & Matched replay establishes h16/h32 IPC crossings while the direct L2 miss rate becomes worse. & Multiple traces, seeds, critical-miss stall cycles, and a fresh held-out window before selecting a final capacity.\\
Strict accuracy & $U/R$ gives request-event yield. & Accepted/filled unique prefetches, duplicate rejection, unused eviction, and pollution.\\
True timeliness & Late merges give one late-failure count. & Issue/fill/demand cycles and lead-time histograms.\\
BPI & One matched-normal index summarizes four cache-side dimensions without allowing surplus compensation. & Direct harmful-eviction/pollution counters and sensitivity analysis before treating BPI as a selection objective.\\
Why IPC rises with more misses & The counters rule out ``more coverage'' and ``better timeliness'' as explanations for h16/h32. & Bandwidth/MSHR occupancy, eviction/pollution, useful-prefetch lead time, and critical-miss stall cycles.\\
