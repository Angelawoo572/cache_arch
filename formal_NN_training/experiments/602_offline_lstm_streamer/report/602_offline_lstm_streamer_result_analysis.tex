\section*{Metrics: formula first}
Let $D=404{,}219$ be post-warmup demand L2 loads, $M$ the method's demand L2 misses, $R$ replayed prefetch-request events, $U$ useful-prefetch demand hits, $L$ demand misses merged into an in-flight prefetch, and $M_0=203{,}131$ no-prefetch L2 demand misses.
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{MR}_{L2}=100\frac{M}{D}}
\qquad
\boxed{A_{\mathrm{proxy}}=100\frac{U}{R}}
\qquad
\boxed{C=100\frac{U}{M_0}}
\qquad
\boxed{T_{\mathrm{late}}=10^5\frac{L}{R}}
\]
Because $D$ is identical across these matched rows, miss rate and miss reduction contain the same ordering information:
\[
\mathrm{MissReduction}=100\frac{M_0-M}{M_0},
\qquad \mathrm{MR}_{L2,0}=100\frac{M_0}{D}=50.2527\%.
\]
\begin{center}\scriptsize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lXl@{}}
\toprule
Metric & What it measures in this experiment & Direction \\
\midrule
$\mathrm{MR}_{L2}$ & Final demand-miss fraction after all prefetch effects. This is the most direct cache-outcome metric shared by live and replayed methods. & Lower \\
$A_{\mathrm{proxy}}$ & Observed useful-hit yield per request event. It is \emph{not} strict hardware accuracy because accepted fills, duplicates, unused lines, and evictions are not separated. & Higher \\
$C$ & Useful demand hits as a fraction of the no-prefetch miss opportunity. & Higher \\
$T_{\mathrm{late}}$ & Late merges per 100k request events. It is a late-density proxy, \emph{not} direct lead time; fewer requests can lower it. & Lower \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\section*{Data $\rightarrow$ insight map}
\begin{center}\scriptsize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.23\textwidth}p{0.35\textwidth}X@{}}
\toprule
Question & Direct evidence & Supported insight \\
\midrule
Why is h8 almost tied? & Relative to streamer, h8 removes only 937 requests (0.058\%), adds 161 L2 misses, raises $\mathrm{MR}_{L2}$ from 3.834\% to 3.874\%, loses 161 useful hits, and improves late density. IPC falls only 0.00011. & h8 nearly reproduces the offline streamer list. Its tiny IPC gap and tiny miss-rate gap consistently indicate a small useful-service loss, not a broad timing failure.\\
Why do wider models lose IPC? & h32--h128 reduce requests by 11--24\%; useful coverage falls to 89.9--90.3\%; IPC falls 0.00229--0.00284. & Wider gates over-prune the stream. The lost opportunity/coverage dominates any reduction in request traffic.\\
Why can $A_{\rm proxy}$ rise while IPC falls? & h64 has the highest $A_{\rm proxy}$ (14.83\%) but lower coverage (89.92\%) and IPC (0.42866). & $U/R$ rewards selective suppression: removing many requests can raise yield while reducing total useful service. Accuracy proxy must be read with coverage.\\
Did stateful TBPTT close the gap? & h8 improves from pre-fix 0.43134 to 0.43139; h128 improves from 0.42787 to 0.42869. No point crosses 0.43150. & Correct recurrence improves some points, especially h128, but threshold/policy behavior still matters more than width.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
