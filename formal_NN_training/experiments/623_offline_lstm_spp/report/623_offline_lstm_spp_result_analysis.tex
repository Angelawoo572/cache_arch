\section*{Why v24 replaces the v23 decoder}
The v23 rank-padded target stream was 93.40\% STOP even though 58.05\% of EVAL
callbacks had one or more teacher actions.  Its five models emitted between 0
and 5,096 requests and recovered at most 0.63\% of teacher actions.  The defect
is therefore the supervised object, not merely hidden-state capacity.

For callback context $h_t$, teacher count $K_t$, and action list $A_t$,
v24 minimizes the natural list negative log likelihood
\[
 -\log P(K_t\mid h_t)
 -\sum_{r=0}^{K_t-1}\log P(a_{t,r}\mid h_t,r).
\]
There are no repeated STOP labels.  $K=0$ is the implicit no-request case.
Count and action cross entropies are unweighted, and deterministic inference
uses count argmax followed by exactly $K$ rank-conditioned action argmaxes.

\section*{Recurrent-core selection}
At hidden size 32, two candidates are trained without loading EVAL:
\begin{itemize}
\item a global chronological LSTM;
\item one chronological hidden/cell state with distinct DEMAND and FILL LSTM transitions selected only by the existing callback-kind bit.
\end{itemize}
The smaller GUARD natural list NLL wins; global wins an exact tie.  Only that
core is then trained at hidden sizes 8, 16, 32, 64, and 128.  Core-ablation
checkpoints are not replayed.

For count classes $K$, joint-action classes $A$, and hidden size $H$, parameter
counts are
\[
N_{\rm global}=9H^2+(74+K+A)H+K+A+1,
\]
\[
N_{\rm routed}=17H^2+(82+K+A)H+K+A+1.
\]

\section*{Required evidence}
Each point reports IPC, L2 miss rate, raw and selected accuracy, coverage,
timeliness, requested/issued/fill counts and fill levels, count confusion and
MAE, target/fill precision and recall, request ratio, count/action entropy, and
action-list identity.  Denominator-zero ratios are N/A rather than numeric
zero.  No composite score is used.

Oracle-count plus NN-action and NN-count plus oracle-action are diagnosis-only
decompositions and are never replayed.  The all-callback TRAIN-modal-delta,
FILL\_LLC policy is a separate non-neural control and is excluded from neural
claims.

\section*{Completion rule}
A completed experiment requires all five neural replay rows, the offline normal
SPP comparator, no-prefetch and live transport references, the non-neural
control, a root PASS with no failures, and the diagnosis files.  PASS proves
contract and accounting integrity; performance conclusions must be made from
the separate measured dimensions above.
