\section*{Metrics: formula first}
Let $D=404{,}219$ be post-warmup demand L2 loads, $M$ the method's demand L2 misses, $R$ replayed prefetch-request events, $U$ useful-prefetch demand hits, $L$ demand misses merged into an in-flight prefetch, and $M_0=203{,}131$ no-prefetch L2 demand misses.
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{MR}_{L2}=100\frac{M}{D}}
\qquad
\boxed{A_{\mathrm{sel}}^{*}=100\frac{U}{R}}
\qquad
\boxed{C=100\frac{U}{M_0}}
\qquad
\boxed{T=100\frac{U}{U+L}}
\]
Because $D$ is identical across these matched rows, miss rate and miss reduction contain the same ordering information:
\[
\mathrm{MissReduction}=100\frac{M_0-M}{M_0},
\qquad \mathrm{MR}_{L2,0}=100\frac{M_0}{D}=50.2527\%.
\]
For an NN point $x$ and its matched offline normal baseline $N$, convert every component to a capped higher-is-better parity ratio:
\[
q_M=\min\!\left(1,\frac{\mathrm{MR}_{L2,N}}{\mathrm{MR}_{L2,x}}\right),\quad
q_A=\min\!\left(1,\frac{A^{*}_{\mathrm{sel},x}}{A^{*}_{\mathrm{sel},N}}\right),\quad
q_C=\min\!\left(1,\frac{C_x}{C_N}\right),\quad
q_T=\min\!\left(1,\frac{T_x}{T_N}\right).
\]
Miss rate and coverage are related cache outcomes, so they first form one block rather than receiving two full weights:
\[
\boxed{Q_{\mathrm{cache}}=\sqrt{q_Mq_C}},\qquad
\boxed{\mathrm{BPI}=100\left(Q_{\mathrm{cache}}q_Aq_T\right)^{1/3}}.
\]
The offline normal baseline is 100. Capping prevents an improvement in one component from hiding a deficit in another. BPI compares NN parity only against its corresponding normal policy; it does not replace IPC or rank different normal-prefetcher families.

\toprule
Metric & What it measures in this experiment & Direction \\
\midrule

\section*{Data $\rightarrow$ insight map}
\begin{center}\scriptsize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.23\textwidth}p{0.34\textwidth}X@{}}
\toprule
Question & Direct evidence & Supported insight \\
\midrule
Why does h8 miss AMPM? & L2 misses rise by 1,734; miss rate rises from 4.218\% to 4.647\%; coverage falls 0.85 points; BPI is 98.10 and IPC falls 0.00138. & h8 is close but does not preserve the normal policy's cache outcome. BPI and IPC consistently identify it as the best NN point.\\
Is timeliness the limiting factor? & Every NN has $T>T_N$; h128 reaches 99.992\%, yet its BPI is 79.76 and IPC is 0.41634. & \textbf{No.} The timing surplus is capped and cannot compensate for miss-rate, accuracy, and coverage losses caused by suppression.\\
Does more LSTM capacity help? & BPI falls from 98.10 (h8) to 79.76 (h128), broadly following miss-rate and coverage degradation. & No monotonic capacity benefit is observed. The bottleneck is policy/label/threshold behavior, not insufficient hidden width.\\
Did stateful TBPTT fix the gap? & Recurrent-state contract is now matched and the run passes, yet no width crosses AMPM. & The previous state mismatch was a real methodology bug, but it was not the cause of the AMPM performance gap.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\section*{What these counters can and cannot prove}
\begin{center}\scriptsize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lXX@{}}
\toprule
Claim type & Supported now & Still required for a causal claim \\
\midrule
Performance & IPC, cycles, L2 demand hits/misses, and matched replay establish the measured ordering. & Multiple traces, seeds, and a fresh held-out window for generalization.\\
Accuracy & $U/R$ compares request-event yield. & Accepted/filled unique prefetches, duplicate rejection, unused eviction, and pollution counters for strict accuracy.\\
Timeliness & $T=U/(U+L)$ uses the explicit late-merge count to compare one failure mode. & Prefetch issue/fill/demand cycles and lead-time histograms to distinguish too-early, on-time, and too-late requests.\\
BPI & h8 retains 98.10\% capped balance parity; wider capacities fall sharply. & Direct harmful-eviction/pollution counters and weight/sensitivity analysis before using BPI as an optimization target.\\
Root cause & h8 loses useful hits and coverage even while measured timeliness improves. & Bandwidth/MSHR pressure, cache pollution, critical-miss stall cycles, and per-target classification errors.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
